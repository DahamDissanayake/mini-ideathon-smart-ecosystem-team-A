To start streaming:
  1. Flash sample-imu-data/sample-imu-data.ino and power the board. A blinking purple LED means it is advertising.
  2. Press Add device… and pick test letGo device from the browser's chooser. You only do this once; it is then remembered in the dropdown.
  3. Press Connect. The board blinks green three times on success.
The chooser lists devices advertising the Nordic UART service. Tick Show all nearby devices to drop that filter.
